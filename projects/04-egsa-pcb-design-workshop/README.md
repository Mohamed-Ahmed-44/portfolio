# Project 04: Spacecraft Electronics & PCB Design Engineering Workshop

## 📌 Overview
An intensive engineering workshop conducted at the **Egyptian Space Agency (EgSA)** (New Cairo, Egypt), focusing on end-to-end design, routing, testing, and manufacturing standards for high-reliability printed circuit boards (PCBs).



---

## 🛰️ Technical Scope & Curriculum

### 1. Electronics Fundamentals & Schematics
* Selection of high-reliability passive and active components adhering to space and industrial operating temperatures.
* Hierarchical schematic design in **Altium Designer** with strict Design Rule Checks (DRC) and component libraries.

### 2. Multi-Layer PCB Layout & High-Speed Routing
* Layer stack-up optimization (signal, ground, and power planes) to minimize ground bounce and cross-talk.
* Differential pair routing and controlled impedance considerations.
* Thermal dissipation strategies including copper pours and thermal relief vias for power transistors and voltage regulators.

### 3. Design for Manufacturing (DFM) & Assembly (DFA)
* Generating standard fabrication deliverables: Gerber RS-274X, NC Drill files, Pick-and-Place centroid files, and Bill of Materials (BOM).
* Solder mask clearances, silkscreen legibility, and fiducial marker placement.
* Hands-on testing, manual SMD soldering techniques, and board bring-up debugging.
